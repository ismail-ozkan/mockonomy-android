Privacy Policy

Mockonomy · Last updated: 16 August 2026

Mockonomy is an HTTP mock server that runs on your own device. It has no backend of its own, no account system, and no analytics.

Data we collect

None. Mockonomy does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no sign-in.

Data the app stores on your device

Everything the app creates stays in its private storage on your device:

  • Your environments and route definitions
  • Response bodies you write or import
  • The request log: requests received by the mock server, together with the responses it returned

This data is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete individual items in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. Note that the request log can contain whatever the app under test sent to the mock server — including headers such as Authorization. That is data you generate on your own device during testing; it never leaves it unless you deliberately export or share it yourself.

Network access

Mockonomy requests the INTERNET permission solely in order to open a listening socket on the loopback interface (127.0.0.1) of your device. The mock server is bound to loopback only: it cannot be reached from your Wi-Fi network or from the internet, and only apps on the same device can connect to it. The app makes no outgoing network requests.

Notifications and the foreground service

While the mock server is running, Mockonomy runs a foreground service and shows a persistent notification with the server status and a Stop action. This keeps the server available while you switch to the app you are testing. The service runs only while you have explicitly started the server, and stops when you stop it. No data is collected for this purpose.

Children

Mockonomy is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data it handles, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
